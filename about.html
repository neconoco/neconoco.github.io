Asako Fujimoto is a sound artist, musician, and composer exploring sonic representations of the environment and algorithmic composition. 

 She began developing her musical background in New York City's vibrant 1990s music scene and works with analog and digital synthesizers, field recordings, and her voice. She has released music and performed both as a solo artist and in various ensembles. 

 Her spatial audio and algorithmic works have been exhibited internationally, often in collaboration with artists from other disciplines. Venues and events include ZKM, the European Capital of Culture, Venice Biennale Architettura, Athens Digital Arts Festival, the Hermitage Museum, and Institut Français du Japon Kansai. 

 Her current practice focuses on sound in new media, audiovisual installations and performances, as well as composition and sound design across diverse productions.
